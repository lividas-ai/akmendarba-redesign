# Responsive visual review

The current Akmendarba enhancement pass preserves the premium template system while adding the two requested functions as first-class parts of the site.

## Stone selection

- Desktop uses a restrained editorial grid with equal card geometry and compact actions.
- Mobile keeps both material cards readable without turning either image into a full-screen panel.
- Favourite and comparison states are visually distinct, keyboard reachable, and persistent.
- The comparison drawer stays available without obscuring the full catalogue; the expanded comparison view is structured as a dialog.
- Only source-evidenced material families are present, so the interface does not imply named stock or availability that Akmendarba did not publish.

## Contact form

- The form aligns to the existing typographic scale, spacing system, line treatments, and neutral palette.
- Field groups follow a clear reading order at desktop, tablet, and narrow mobile widths.
- Inline validation is placed next to the relevant field and focus moves to the first invalid control.
- Selected stones are carried into the enquiry context and shown before submission.
- The completion state is honest: it offers a local summary, copy action, and prefilled email rather than pretending a backend submission occurred.

## Verification

The visual captures were generated after successful interaction tests at desktop, tablet, mobile, and `320 px` narrow-mobile widths. All tested routes remained within the viewport, and the complete production/static build pipeline passed.
